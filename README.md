# Procurement_analysis
**Introduction**

Procurement plays an important role in an organization's operational efficiency and financial performance. Effective procurement management helps organizations control purchasing costs, maintain supplier quality, ensure timely deliveries, and achieve cost savings. This project focuses on analyzing procurement data using Microsoft Excel to evaluate purchasing trends, supplier performance, delivery delays, defective units, and savings opportunities.

**Problem Statement**

Organizations often deal with large volumes of procurement data, making it difficult to monitor purchasing activities and identify operational inefficiencies. The project aims to highlight:

1. Monthly procurement expenditure.
2. Identify high-spending item categories.
3. Delays in supplier deliveries affecting operational efficiency.
4.Variations in supplier quality and defective product rates.
The objective is to analyze procurement data and identify areas that require improvement through an interactive dashboard.

**Dashboard Goals**

The primary goals of this dashboard are:

1.Monitor Procurement Expenditure.
2.Evaluate Supplier Performance.
3.Track Delivery Performance.
4.Measure Cost Savings.
5.Analyze Category-wise Spending

**Key Insights**

1.Procurement Expenditure: Total procurement spending stands at $45.20 million across 775 purchase orders.
2.Cost Savings: The organization has achieved approximately $3.91 million in procurement savings, indicating opportunities to optimize purchasing costs through negotiation.
3.Monthly Spending Trends: March records the highest monthly procurement expenditure, while May shows comparatively lower spending.
4.Category-wise Spending: Raw Materials and Electronics are among the major contributors to procurement expenditure.
5.Supplier Quality: Delta Logistics has the highest defect rate among the suppliers displayed, followed by Beta Supplies. These suppliers may require closer quality monitoring.
6.Delivery Performance: Late deliveries exceed on-time deliveries in the displayed comparison, indicating a potential area for supplier performance improvement.
7.Monthly Savings Trends: March records the highest monthly savings, while May shows the lowest savings among the displayed months.
8.Category-wise Savings: MRO and Office Supplies appear to generate relatively higher savings compared with the other categories.
